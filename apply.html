NOW BOARDING: CREW

Staff applications.

The Refuge is run by its members. If you want to help keep the Ark moving, these are the roles we're looking for.

ROLE · MODERATION

Moderator

Keep the Ark welcoming and safe, help members when they need it, and lead by example. Above-average moderation, run by people, not bots.

A real time commitment. Staff from other departments get priority.

ROLE · EVENTS

Events Creator

Start and run your own events. Bring a podcast, stream your games, or run something nobody's tried yet.

A lighter process: just a proposal to get approved.

ROLE · RECRUITMENT

Recruiter

Be an ambassador for the Refuge: welcome newcomers, help with social media, and track activity and retention.

For many of our leaders, this was the first staff role on the way to bigger things.

Every role comes with training, one-on-one leadership support, and a team working toward the same goals. Staff positions are unpaid, so only apply if you can commit to the workload.
